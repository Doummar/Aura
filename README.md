<p>
Aura adds a <b>minimal live focus HUD</b> to your Anki review sessions, helping you stay aware of your pacing, focus rhythm, and study consistency without cluttering your workspace.
</p>

<p>
Built around a calm and distraction-free philosophy, Aura quietly tracks your session while giving you lightweight feedback through live timers, break states, and focus indicators.
</p>

<p>
<b>Features:</b>
</p>

<ul>
    <li>Minimal floating HUD during Anki reviews</li>
    <li>Live study session timer</li>
    <li>Focus score based on answer rhythm and consistency</li>
    <li>Cards remaining counter</li>
    <li>Automatic study / break cycle management</li>
    <li>Support for both short and long breaks</li>
    <li>Break completion notifications</li>
    <li>Quick pause and resume directly from the toolbar</li>
    <li>Session resumes from the exact same state after pause</li>
    <li>Adjustable score sensitivity</li>
    <li>Calibration system for more stable scoring</li>
    <li>Custom study, short break, and long break durations</li>
    <li>Minimal toolbar designed for low distraction</li>
    <li>Automatic dark / light mode support</li>
</ul>

<p>
<b>How to use:</b>
</p>

<ul>
    <li>Start reviewing cards normally in Anki</li>
    <li>Aura automatically activates during review sessions</li>
    <li>Monitor your live timer, score, and remaining cards</li>
    <li>Click the timer text to pause the session</li>
    <li>Click again to instantly resume</li>
    <li>Take automatic short and long breaks when prompted</li>
    <li>Open <b>Tools → Aura Settings</b> to customise behaviour</li>
</ul>
